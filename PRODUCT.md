# Notchy
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Native macOS 13+, personal Apple Silicon use.

## Stack
Approved: Swift 5.9 SwiftPM executable, AppKit NSPanel and SwiftUI ObservableObject. Bundled, ad-hoc signed app; no automatic installation.

## Users and purpose
A personal MacBook companion at the camera cutout, offering music, synced lyrics, focus, water, file shelf, opt-in memory-only clipboard, tasks and calculated prayer times.

## Constraints
Independent original implementation. Leave Personal Guard untouched. No copied Nusa assets or source. Bundle BSD-3-Clause mediaremote-adapter v0.7.7, launched with system Perl, never linked. Local atomic JSON persistence; no accounts or cloud. Lyrics metadata goes to LRCLIB only after opt-in. Jakarta coordinates are an editable preset, not detected location. Prayer reminders require location confirmation. No Accessibility/camera/location permission requests or notification scraping.

## Brand commitments
User-approved black camera-integrated shell, system font and SF Symbols, white text, restrained green progress, brief mascot wake response. No decorative gradients or perpetual animation. Working name Notchy, no trademark clearance claim.

## Accessibility and success
Keyboard-accessible panel and settings, visible focus, Reduce Motion, readable errors, camera clearance. Real native and media integration evidence required; report hardware/permission blockers rather than claim simulated proof.
